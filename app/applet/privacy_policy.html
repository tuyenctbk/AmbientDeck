Privacy Policy for AmbientDeck Version 1.0

Last Updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AmbientDeck: Smart TV Wall & Kiosk ("we", "our", or "the application") operates with respect to user information, privacy, and public data streams.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

AmbientDeck is designed with privacy-first principles. We do not collect, harvest, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not require accounts, sign-ins, phone numbers, or email addresses to use the application.

2. Permissions and Hardware Usage

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required exclusively to stream real-time public telemetry data, including weather forecasts, air quality indices, open cryptocurrency market tickers, overhead public flight states, and historical trivia.
  • Leanback / Android TV Support: Used to render user interface layouts optimized for 10-foot television remote navigation and touchscreen kiosks.

3. Public APIs and Third-Party Data Sources

AmbientDeck communicates directly with public, zero-key endpoints to render dashboard widgets:

  • Open-Meteo: For atmospheric forecasts and Air Quality Index (AQI) values based on chosen geographic coordinates. No tracking identifiers are transmitted.
  • Binance Public APIs: For public 24-hour market prices and percentage trends of major digital assets.
  • OpenSky Network: For public ADS-B flight positional data within a regional bounding box.
  • MuffinLabs: For daily historical milestone trivia.
  • Unsplash: For streaming high-resolution 4K wallpaper imagery.

4. On-Device Storage and Preferences

All user preferences (including selected city, active widgets, temperature units, and layout mode) are stored locally on your device via standard Android SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

AmbientDeck does not target or knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. The application is an ambient utility suitable for all ages without advertisements or trackers.

6. Policy Updates

Any revisions to this Privacy Policy will be reflected on this page with an updated modification date.

7. Contact

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at support@ambientdeck.app.
