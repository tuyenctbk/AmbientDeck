AMBIENTDECK Smart TV Wall & Kiosk

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026 • Version 1.0
Privacy First Guarantee: AmbientDeck is built with zero tracking. We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal user data, advertising identifiers, location telemetry, or device fingerprints.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

AmbientDeck adheres to a strict data minimization model:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account logins.
  • No Location Tracking: The app does not access device GPS sensors in the background. Geographic presets or custom coordinates chosen by the user remain strictly local.
  • No Advertising or Tracking SDKs: There are no advertising trackers, behavioral profiling scripts, or analytics brokers integrated into AmbientDeck.

2. Public External Services & Network Requests

To provide real-time ambient information, AmbientDeck queries public informational APIs directly from your device:

  • Open-Meteo API: Retrieves weather conditions and Air Quality Index (AQI) values for selected city coordinates without transmitting personal identifiers.
  • OpenSky Network: Retrieves public transponder flight broadcasts overhead for live airspace radar mapping.
  • Binance Public Market API: Retrieves 24-hour ticker exchange rates and price trends for selected crypto assets.
  • MuffinLabs API: Retrieves curated historical events and milestones corresponding to the current day.
  • Unsplash Curated CDN: Delivers high-definition 4K photography and wallpapers for ambient background presentation.

3. Local Device Storage

User preferences (including selected display layout, temperature and speed units, active widget toggles, and OLED burn-in mitigation preferences) are stored strictly on your local device via Android SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device.

4. OLED Screen Protection & Display Safety

AmbientDeck includes active pixel drift algorithms designed to periodically shift on-screen elements to protect OLED and AMOLED televisions from screen burn-in during continuous kiosk operation.

5. Children's Privacy

AmbientDeck does not target or knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. The application is completely safe for all audiences.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect platform updates. Any updates will be posted with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at support@ambientdeck.app.
